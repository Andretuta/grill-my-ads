# Offer & awareness

## Hormozi value equation
`Value = (Dream outcome × Perceived likelihood of achievement) / (Time delay × Effort & sacrifice)`
Source: alexhormozi.wiki/frameworks/value-equation-explained [PR]

Grill each lever separately and score 1–5:
| Lever | Question | How to raise it |
|---|---|---|
| Dream outcome ↑ | "What does life look like after buying, in their words?" | Name the specific result, not the feature |
| Perceived likelihood ↑ | "Why would they believe it works for them?" | Proof: numbers, testimonials, demo, guarantee, authority |
| Time delay ↓ | "How fast is the first win?" | Show the first result in minutes/days; quick-start bonus |
| Effort & sacrifice ↓ | "What do they have to do / give up?" | Done-for-you, templates, no-commitment trial |

Offer enhancers: bonuses that kill objections, guarantee (conditional / unconditional), scarcity (real only), urgency (real deadlines only), naming (memorable offer name). **Fake scarcity/urgency = policy risk ("deceptive practices").**

## Schwartz — 5 awareness levels
Source: succession.media/guides/schwartz-awareness-levels-meta-ads [PR]

| Level | Buyer knows… | Ad opens with | Typical format | Funnel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Nothing about the problem | Story, curiosity, relatable scene | UGC story, advertorial | TOFU |
| Problem-aware | The pain, not solutions | Name the pain precisely | PAS short video | TOFU |
| Solution-aware | Solutions exist, not yours | Your unique mechanism | Demo, comparison | MOFU |
| Product-aware | Your product, not convinced | Proof, objections, social proof | Testimonials, reviews, FAQ ad | MOFU/BOFU |
| Most aware | Ready, needs a reason now | The offer + deadline | Offer card, carousel | BOFU / retargeting |

**Rule:** tag every concept with its awareness level; cold audiences (Advantage+ broad) need at least one Unaware/Problem-aware concept. Cover ≥2 levels across the 3–5 concepts.

## Motivators (for creative diversity)
Pick different motivators per concept: save money · save time · status · belonging · fear of missing out · health/safety · curiosity · convenience · identity ("for people like me"). Different motivators unlock different audiences under Andromeda.
